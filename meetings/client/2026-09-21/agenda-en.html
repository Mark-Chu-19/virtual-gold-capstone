A · Use case: confirm the direction

10 min

On Sep 15 the client asked: a vertical financial-services use case, or a horizontal sensitive-data sanitization application? Inderpal proposed a trading assistant on public filings. Our team proposal from Sep 18:

  • Both. The sanitization gateway is the horizontal capability, measurable on its own (leak rate, re-identification success). The trading assistant is the vertical scenario that runs on top of it: public filings and market data may go to the cloud; positions and strategy never leave.
  • Why this vertical: the client proposed it, all data is public (SEC EDGAR, market prices, FinQA benchmark), and the local-vs-cloud split has an obvious business reason.
  • Scope guard: a question-answering assistant over filings with confidential context. No order execution, no live feeds, no backtesting.
  • Ask: does this direction work for you, or do you want horizontal only or vertical only?

B · Sensitivity gate: how the system decides what leaves

Yudi · 20 min

Alex's question from Sep 15. Yudi presents briefing-security-deidentification-en.html (this folder). The points to land:

  • Sensitive is not the same as PII. "Exit the regional-bank book before the Fed meeting" has no identifier and discloses the whole strategy. We protect intent, strategy, positions, then identifiers, in that order.
  • Four layers, only the last one is a judgement: source (position store is confidential by construction, EDGAR is public by construction), pattern (checksums and fixed formats), written policy (one page, reviewable by the client), and judgement only for the trader's free-text notes, which are dropped by default.
  • Two scores, not one gate: sensitivity and task-necessity, driving keep / abstract / remove. A sensitivity-only gate keeps 4% of task-critical content; two scores keep 95% (MINIM, ICML 2026).
  • Alex's three alternatives, partial restriction, output censorship, split routing: all in the design; split routing is evaluated in weeks 9–11, the midpoint shows whole-request classification.
  • Three design conclusions: whatever authorizes a release is deterministic code, never a model (a model can be argued into anything by text it reads); the disclosure rules are fixed from the question before retrieval, so text planted in a filing cannot steer them; and disclosure is budgeted across the whole session, since twenty harmless questions can still reveal the book.
  • Measurement, five separate numbers rather than one accuracy: per-category detection weighted toward misses, leak rate at the network boundary with planted canaries, answer quality with protection on vs off, attack success vs unsafe actions counted separately, and disclosure across a whole session.
  • Ask Alex: is this the review he wanted, and what should the written policy page cover for his use?

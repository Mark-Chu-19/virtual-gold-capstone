B · How the service works

Zhexuan & Anmol · 12 min

The service sits between your system and the cloud assistant. It never calls the cloud itself and needs no outbound network access — what happens to the redacted text afterwards is entirely your system's decision.

The shape of it
Your system posts the user's task plus the email. We return the same content with private spans replaced by placeholders, plus a decision for the request as a whole. Later, separately, you can post the cloud's reply back and we restore the real values. The mapping from placeholder to original never leaves our machine.
Fail closed, everywhere
Any error means the content was not processed. A successful response that is not marked ready carries the same consequence: it must not be forwarded to the cloud. If a component fails, nothing is released.
Nothing sensitive is logged
Input text, detected values and restored text are never written to logs — only ids, counts, timings, decisions and error types.
Detail: the endpoints and what each returns
EndpointWhat it does
POST /v1/redactionsDetect, redact, and return a decision for the whole request
GET /v1/redactions/{id}Check status, retrieve a result
POST /v1/redactions/{id}/restoreScan the cloud's reply, then restore the real values
DELETE /v1/redactions/{id}Delete a record
GET /v1/info · /healthz · /readyzService metadata and health checks

Bearer auth on everything except the health checks. Errors are RFC 9457 problem details. Full contract in docs/research/zhexuan/2026-09-27/.

C · Deciding what to redact

Yudi · 18 min

Two designs, presented together, because they are not alternatives — one is the baseline the other has to beat.

1 · The baseline: mask everything we find

Start by not deciding at all. If any detector flags a span, it is replaced with a reversible placeholder. The public support line gets masked too. Nothing is judged, nothing is argued about.

Why this works as a starting point
Masking a public phone number used to be a mistake because the information was lost. Here it is only hidden from the cloud and comes back in the final answer. So over-redaction stops being a visible error and becomes a small, measurable drop in the cloud's reasoning quality. The hardest judgment call in the project turns into a tuning knob.
And it is the control condition
Masking everything sets the ceiling on safety and the floor on usefulness. Every refinement after this has to earn its place by measurement — how much answer quality it buys back, and how much leakage it costs. Starting strict and loosening with evidence is easier to stand behind than starting loose.

2 · The refinement: let more through, but only where it is safe

Masking everything is safe but blunt. The refinement adds two judgements on top, so that public and low-risk content can pass through intact.

JudgementWhat it asksEffect
CategoryWhat kind of thing is this?Sets how careful we are with it
Can the model decide?May a confidence score release this at all?For five categories: never. Policy only

The five that a score may never release: medical, financial accounts, government IDs, credentials, business-confidential. Everything else is score-driven, with a stricter bar for personal details and a looser one for public ones.

Detail: the category list, and where it comes from
CategoryCoversScore may release?
medicalconditions, diagnoses, appointmentsNo
financial_accountaccount and card numbersNo
government_idnational ID, passport, licenceNo
credentialpasswords, keys, tokensNo
business_confidentialunreleased figures, deal terms, project code names, legal mattersNo
person_private · contact_personalprivate individuals, their phone, email, addressYes · strict bar
person_public · contact_publicpublic figures, published support linesYes · loose bar
org · location · date_time · demographic · quantitycompanies, places, dates, age and gender, amountsYes · loose bar

Impact levels follow NIST SP 800-122. The rule that low-risk items tighten up when several appear together follows Sweeney's finding that ZIP code, gender and date of birth uniquely identify an estimated 87% of the US population. Business-confidential is the one category with neither a format nor a fixed meaning, which is why we ask for a term list below.

3 · When the model is unsure

It masks anyway, and records that it was unsure. No request waits on a person. Those records are how we measure over-redaction, so the cost of caution is a number rather than a guess.

Where does the addendum with Diane at CMU stand?

Building a standalone service with an HTTP endpoint, rather than integrating directly, was explicitly the workaround while this is pending. Two things turn on it:

  • Whether the design stays as it is, or changes once we can see your architecture
  • Whether the API request can tell us where each piece of text came from — body, quoted thread, the user's own words. We only need that coarse level, not anything about your internal structures, and it would measurably reduce our error rate. It may not be blocked by the NDA at all, which is worth checking.

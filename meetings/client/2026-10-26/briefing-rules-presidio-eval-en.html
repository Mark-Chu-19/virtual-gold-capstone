Rules + Presidio: evaluation and fixes (Oct 25)

Process

  • Dataset: ai4privacy pii-masking-400k, English validation set (synthetic, US + UK). Local only; its licence bars redistribution
  • Mapping: 16 of its 17 labels mapped to our types (USERNAME has no match)
  • Scope: only types that the tier table assigns to rules or Presidio
  • Match: a labelled span counts as found if any detected span overlaps it

Where the fixes sit

  • Union means a false positive from any path survives, so each path filters its own
  • A wrong type is only dangerous when it is a lower tier: a Low item seen alone is kept, so it leaks
PathHandlesSo we do not
Rules / PresidioFixed formats: email, phone, card, ID and account numbers–
LLMContext: loose address parts, company vs person, business secrets, healthHand-write rules for every context case in rules / Presidio

  • Precision is understated: the dataset leaves IMEI, passport, crypto and company names unlabelled
  • ADDRESS_HOME: the dataset labels house number, street and ZIP separately; our rule needs a full US address, and spaCy tags the parts as CITY / DATE
  • CARD_NUMBER: the dataset's card numbers are random (only 10% pass Luhn), and our extra "cards" are unlabelled IMEIs, so this is not a real measure
  • Rules alone: email 100%, GOV_ID 32%, phone 28%. Recall comes mostly from Presidio
